SnapJournal Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

The short version

  • SnapJournal does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your journal entries, photos and locations are stored only on your device. We never receive, see or store them.
  • The camera, gallery and location are used only when you tap the matching button, and only to add to your own entry.
  • SnapJournal has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and does not sell or share any data.
  • You can delete your data at any time from inside the app or by uninstalling it.

1. Who we are

SnapJournal ("the app", "we", "us") is a personal photo journal app for Android developed by Peter Ngacha. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

2. Information the app stores

SnapJournal saves the following information that you choose to add, locally on your device only:

  • The text of your journal entries and the mood you select
  • Photos you take with the camera or pick from your gallery to attach to an entry (a copy is saved in the app's private storage)
  • The location you choose to tag on an entry: its coordinates and a place name such as the city and country
  • The date and time each entry was created or edited
  • Your settings, such as whether daily reminders are on and the reminder time

This information never leaves your device through SnapJournal. We have no servers that receive it and no way to access it.

3. Permissions and how they are used

  • Camera — used only when you tap Camera to take a photo for an entry.
  • Photos — when you tap Gallery, Android's photo picker lets you choose a single photo. The app does not get access to the rest of your gallery.
  • Location (approximate and precise) — used only while the app is open and only when you tap Add Location. Your location is never tracked in the background. To show a place name, your phone's built-in location service converts the coordinates into a city and country name.
  • Notifications — used only if you turn on Daily Reminder in Settings, to remind you to write an entry. Reminders are scheduled on your device.

You can refuse or withdraw any permission at any time in your phone's Settings. The rest of the app keeps working without it.

4. Information we do not collect

SnapJournal does not collect personal details (name, email, phone), contacts, audio, files outside the app, device identifiers, advertising IDs or usage analytics. The app does not contain advertising or third-party tracking tools.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your information with anyone, because we never receive it.

6. Device backups

If you have turned on Android's built-in backup, your phone may include SnapJournal's data in your personal Google account backup so it can be restored on a new device. This backup is controlled by you and Google, not by us. You can manage it in your phone's Settings under Google → Backup.

7. Keeping or deleting your data

Your entries stay on your device until you remove them. You can:

  • Delete an individual entry, together with its photo, from the entry's screen in the app
  • Delete all entries and photos with Settings → Clear All Entries in the app
  • Remove all SnapJournal data in Settings → Apps → SnapJournal → Storage → Clear data
  • Uninstall the app, which permanently deletes all of its data from your device

8. Security

Your entries and photos are stored in the app's private storage area on your device, which other apps cannot read. To protect them, we recommend using a screen lock on your phone.

9. Children's privacy

SnapJournal is a general journaling app and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how SnapJournal handles information (for example, by adding cloud sync), we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.

11. Contact us

For any question about this policy or your privacy, email info@fastweb.co.ke.
